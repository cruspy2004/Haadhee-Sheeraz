Helps creators 5x revenue by monetizing content through software products.

Live: h1grow.store

## How HR reads it

The claim is doing all the work and none of it is yours yet. "5x revenue"
is a marketing promise with no named customer, no number you measured, and
no description of what the software actually *does*.

Right now a hiring manager reads: "landing page."

## What it needs

Answer, in one line each:
- What does it actually do? A tool? A course? A service?
- Who used it? Even "3 creators" beats an unnamed 5x.
- What did **you** build versus what did you position?

If it's mostly a landing page, say so and move it down the list — an
honest small thing outranks an unverifiable big one.

## Why it matters anyway

It's your only piece of evidence for "took a product from nothing to
live," which is the core of [[Lane - Product Engineer]]. Worth the effort
to make it concrete.
