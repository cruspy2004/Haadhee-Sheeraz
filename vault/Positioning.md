# Positioning

## The pitch

> I build products end-to-end, and I'm good at explaining them.

That is the sentence both lanes buy. [[Lane - Product Engineer]] hears
"ships alone, no hand-holding." [[Lane - DevRel]] hears "engineer who can
actually communicate."

## What it replaces

"A simple, reliable builder" is humble and memorable but says nothing a
hiring manager can act on. It doesn't tell them what you build, who for,
or why you. → [[Site Copy]]

## The proof stack

One item per claim. More than one is padding.

| Claim | Proof |
|---|---|
| I ship real backend | [[Watify]] — 5,000 users |
| I ship whole products | [[H1GROW]] |
| I can reach developers | [[JavascriptPeter]] — 3M views |
| I automate with AI | [[JavascriptPeter]] pipeline, [[TopicPulse]] |
| I'm employable now | [[FlyRank AI]] |

## The thing that makes you unusual

Almost nobody has **both** [[Watify]] and [[JavascriptPeter]]. Engineers
who can build an audience are rare; audience-builders who can write a
Postgres migration are rarer. Lead with the combination, not with either
half.

Stop apologising for the mix. The mix is the position.
