# Leetly

Growth Engineer. Nov 2024 — Jan 2025. US-based edtech, mobile DSA product.
Drove adoption through data-driven growth experiments.

## How HR reads it

Two months is short enough to read as a trial or a contract, so it won't
carry weight on its own.

The title is also a problem. "Growth Engineer" sounds inflated unless you
can name the engineering — what did you *build*? If the answer is
"experiments and analysis," that's a growth **analyst**, and claiming
engineer invites a question you can't answer well.

## What it's actually good for

Not as a headline role. As supporting evidence for one specific claim in
both lanes: **you understand funnels and adoption, not just features.**

For [[Lane - Product Engineer]] that's the difference between an engineer
who builds what's on the ticket and one who asks whether anyone will use
it. Startups pay for the second.

For [[Lane - DevRel]] it's directly the job — developer adoption *is* a
funnel.

## Fix

Name one experiment and its result. One concrete example rescues the whole
entry.
