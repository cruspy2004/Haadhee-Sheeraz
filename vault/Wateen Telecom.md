## How HR reads it

The 5,000-applicant filter is the most useful sentence in your entire
résumé, because it's a judgement *someone else* made about you. Self-
description gets discounted; external selection doesn't.

Large recognisable employer + competitive filter + real shipped system is
a complete story. This is the entry that gets you the interview.

## Careful

The description is written as **learning**: "learned to work with PERN,"
"learned to optimize." That's honest but it positions you as a student
rather than a contributor.

Rewrite in the past tense of things done, not things learned. "Handled
auth, schema and migrations" says the same thing and sounds like an
engineer.
