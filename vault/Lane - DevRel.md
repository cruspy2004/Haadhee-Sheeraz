# Lane - DevRel

Developer Advocate / Developer Relations.

## The job

Build demos and sample apps. Write tutorials. Make videos. Answer
developers in Discord and on X. Carry what they complain about back to the
product team. Half engineering, half communication, all public.

## Why you fit — and this is the strong one

Most DevRel candidates are one of these two and bluffing the other:

- can build → [[Watify]], [[H1GROW]], [[FlyRank AI]]
- can reach developers → [[JavascriptPeter]], 7.5k followers and 3M views
  in two months, **teaching JavaScript specifically**

You are genuinely both. That is the whole job description and it is rare.

## What they screen for

1. **Proof you can hold an audience** → [[JavascriptPeter]] is unusually
   strong evidence. Most applicants have a dead blog.
2. **Proof you can actually code** → [[Watify]]
3. **Can you teach?** → your content already is teaching. Say so.
4. **Public speaking** → nothing yet → [[Gaps]]

## Where to apply

Devtool and API companies — the ones whose customers are developers.
Databases, auth, payments, AI infra, CI/CD. They hire DevRel constantly and
struggle to find people who can do both halves.

## The reframe

Stop filing [[JavascriptPeter]] under "content" or "side project". For
this lane it is your **primary** work experience. It is a developer
education channel you built, automated and scaled.
