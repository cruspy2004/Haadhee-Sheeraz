# Site Copy

What the portfolio should say once [[Positioning]] is settled.

## Hero

Currently: *"A simple, reliable builder."*

Memorable, humble, and it tells a hiring manager nothing they can act on.
It also undersells — nothing about 5,000 applicants or 3M views is simple.

Options that keep the tone but carry information:

- "I build products end-to-end, and I explain them well."
- "I build things and teach people how they work."
- "Full-stack engineer. 3M views teaching JavaScript."

The third is the most aggressive and the most likely to get a reply,
because it states both halves of [[Positioning]] in seven words.

Keep the current line if you like it — but put a second line under it that
does the work.
