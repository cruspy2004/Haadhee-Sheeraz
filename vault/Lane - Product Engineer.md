# Lane - Product Engineer

Full-stack product engineer at an early startup.

## The job

Take a feature from idea to production alone. Frontend, backend, deploy,
then watch users use it. Small team, high ownership, no specialist to hand
off to.

## Why you fit

- [[Watify]] — real backend at scale, not a tutorial project
- [[H1GROW]] — a whole product, not a component
- [[FlyRank AI]] — currently employed doing this
- [[Karobar Box]] — you've been on the other side of the table

## What they screen for

1. **Can you ship without supervision?** → [[Watify]] answers this
2. **Do you understand users, not just tickets?** → [[JavascriptPeter]]
   answers this, unusually well
3. **Can you read a codebase you didn't write?** → nothing answers this
   yet → [[Gaps]]

## Where to apply

Seed / Series A startups, 5–30 people. Roles titled "Product Engineer",
"Full-Stack Engineer", "Founding Engineer" (junior). Avoid enterprise —
they want specialists and will read your range as unfocused.

## What kills the application

A skills list of 35 technologies. At a startup the interviewer *is* the
CTO and will pick the one you know least. → [[Gaps]]
