# Karobar Box

Fintech — instant transaction validation with audiovisual confirmation.
Incubated at NIC Pakistan, selected 1 of 30 from 570. Built as a lean
startup.

**Removed from the portfolio site** (your call, Aug 2026).

## How HR reads it

The selection stat is genuinely strong — 1 of 30 from 570 is a real filter
someone else applied to you, and third-party validation always outranks
self-description.

But as a *project* it's thin: no live product to show, and "fintech" plus
"lean startup" tells an engineer nothing about what you built.

## Where it still belongs

Not as a project. As a **line in your story** for [[Lane - Product
Engineer]]: you've taken something from idea through an incubator, so you
understand why startups kill features and ship ugly.

Keep it on the résumé. Keep it off the site. Use it in interviews when
asked "tell me about a time you built something from nothing."
